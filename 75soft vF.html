The Five

Five small things, every day, for seventy-five days.

Daily Habits

  • Out of bed by 7:30 AM (weekdays only — weekends are rest days)
  • Walk 10,000 steps
  • No Instagram or social media — zero, all day
  • Read 10 pages of your current book
  • Morning pages: gratitude, intention, affirmation, plus the day's prompt

The Soft Rules

  • One missed day per week is free — life happens
  • Each additional miss in a week adds one day to your finish date
  • You never reset to day one. Ever.
  • Weekends count as complete days even though the morning habit is skipped
  • The goal is showing up consistently, not perfectly
