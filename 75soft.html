The Five

Five small things, every day, for seventy-five days.

Daily Habits

  • Out of bed by 7:30 AM (no phone in bed first)
  • Walk 10,000 steps
  • No Instagram or social media — zero, all day
  • Read 10 pages of your current book
  • Morning pages: gratitude, intention, affirmation, plus the day's prompt

The Soft Rules

  • One missed day per week is allowed — life happens
  • Two misses in a week adds a day to the end
  • You never reset to day one. Ever.
  • The goal is 75 days of mostly hitting all five, not 75 days of perfection
